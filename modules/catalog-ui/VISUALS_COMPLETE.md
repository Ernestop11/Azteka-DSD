## 🔥 Highlights

### Confetti Celebration
50 animated particles falling from top on order success:
- Random neon colors
- Glow effects
- Random trajectories
- Auto-hide after 5 seconds
- **Performance**: Negligible impact

### Dynamic Neon Colors
Savings badge color changes based on percentage:
- **50%+** → Magenta (best deal!)
- **30-49%** → Purple (great deal)
- **20-29%** → Orange (good deal)
- **10-19%** → Lime (decent savings)
- **<10%** → Cyan (small savings)

### Glossy Overlays
Semi-transparent gradient overlays creating depth:
- Top: Light glossy effect
- Bottom: Reflection shadow
- Result: Premium, polished look

### Parallax Hero
Background moves slower than foreground:
- Creates depth illusion
- Smooth on scroll
- GPU-accelerated
- Disabled on low-end devices
